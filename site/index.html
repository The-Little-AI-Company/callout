Call out the BS.

Highlight something suspicious and hit the key. I'll bring the receipts.

A small Windows tray app. Press one key over anything you are reading and get an honest read: what the text is doing to you, which of its claims hold up against real sources, and which are lies. Sources shown for every verdict. Unsure is a real answer.

Two lanes, one keypress.

The fast lane reads the shape of the text in under a second. The deep lane fetches sources and checks each claim. A meter at the top says how much of it is BS, in words.

Fast lane

01

Under a second. What the text's own shape says: pushes urgency, big numbers with no source, contradicts itself, attacks people instead of the point. Plus how it is trying to persuade you, from thirty-four techniques, each with the sentence that shows it.

Header on every result Signals in the text itself. Not a truth check.

Deep lane

02

A few seconds. Each claim gets a traffic light and a one-line why. Green needs a fetched source that supports it. Red needs a contradicting source, a quote missing from the source it cites, or a call from the helper's general knowledge, always labelled as such.

Every verdict comes with the passage and the link.

BS meter

03

Computed from the verdicts, never from vibes. Pants on fire with a flame when nearly all of it is false. Smoke when most of it is. A halo when it holds up. Mixed in between. Words on screen, never a percentage.

Write-up two plain sentences, checked against the verdict list before you see them.

The honesty rules.

The app is allowed to be funny while it loads. It is not allowed to be funny instead of being unsure.

No verdict without a source
Green needs a supporting source and no contradiction. Sourced red needs a contradicting source or a fabricated quote.
Helper calls are labelled
When sources are silent, the AI helper gives its call from general knowledge. It shows as a hollow dot with the words "no source fetched". It never overrides a sourced verdict.
Unsure names its reason
No source found. Source paywalled. Evidence off-topic. Judgment not confident. Every gray row says which.
Checked twice
The helper extracts claims and writes the summary. A separate judgment model checks every sentence it writes before display.
Words, not numbers
Confidence shows as strong evidence, some evidence, or can't tell. The meter shows a label, not a score.

Your keys, your machine.

Callout has no server and no account. You add up to three API keys once, in Settings, with a test button for each. They live in the Windows credential store. Text you check goes only to those services and to the pages Callout fetches for evidence.

TypeSafe
Required. The judgment model, Jev. Powers the fast lane and every verdict. Get a key
Tavily
Optional. Web search for the deep lane. Without it, only sources linked in the text are checked. Get a key
LLM helper
Optional. DeepSeek by default, any OpenAI-compatible or Anthropic endpoint works. Extracts claims, reads screenshots, answers questions, writes the summary, and gives its own call where sources are silent. Get a key
Cost
Whatever your providers charge. Settings shows calls and tokens per API, always on, never sent anywhere.
